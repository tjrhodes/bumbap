Steps

Pick a part, then tap its keys to switch steps on and off. The small LED on each part flashes when it plays.

Drag a key up or down to set how hard it hits (a new step starts at 85, so it can go louder or much quieter). Drag it sideways to push it slightly early or late: at most 1/24 of a beat. The small tab above a key plays that step backwards: it swells in and ends exactly as the next step begins.

Each part has its own Level, Pitch (±12 semitones; higher also plays shorter, like a sampler) and Length (how soon it fades out). Parts the 808 set recorded at several settings also get their real 808 knobs: Tone and Decay on the kick, Tone and Snappy on the snare, Decay on the open hat, Tuning on the toms. Each click of those knobs is a different recording; the display shows its 808 setting, e.g. 2550. The M tab above each part mutes it.

Knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or use the arrow keys; double-tap to reset. Keyboard: space plays and stops, 1–8 pick a part, arrows on a focused key change its level and nudge.

No sound on an iPhone? Turn the ring/silent switch off; Safari mutes web audio with it.
